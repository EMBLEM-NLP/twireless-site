Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you send us through this website.

What we collect

When you send a repair or visit request, we receive your name, phone number and the details you type into the form. The website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why we collect it

We use your details only to reply to your request and arrange your visit or repair. We don't sell your details or share them for marketing.

Where it's kept

Form requests are delivered to the store through our website host's form service. We delete requests we no longer need.

The map

The map on our pages starts as a simple sketch. If you press "Show the map", your browser loads Google's map, and Google can see that visit under its own privacy policy. Until you press it, the page sends nothing to Google.

Your account with your provider

Plans and services you buy from Bell, Virgin Plus or Lucky Mobile are covered by that provider's own privacy policy.
