What you can set up

  • Bell Internet

    Internet plans for your home, including fibre where Bell's fibre network reaches your address.

    Bell TV delivered by satellite dish.

    A home phone line, on its own or with internet and TV.

Watch Fibe TV on the screens you have

The Fibe TV app plays live and on-demand TV on the Bell Streamer, Apple TV, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV and Google Chromecast.
