Bell, Virgin Plus or Lucky Mobile?

One network sits behind all three. What sets them apart is how you pay and what you get with the plan.

Which of these sounds like you?

  • You’d rather pay first, with no credit check: Lucky Mobile
  • You want a monthly plan with some perks built in: Virgin Plus
  • You’re after a new phone, paid off month by month: Bell
  • You need internet, TV or a home phone line: Bell at home
  • You’re buying phones for a team: Bell for business

Already with Virgin Plus?

Two dated changes affect people who are already customers. Check whether either one is yours.

Prepaid ends on January 15, 2027

On a prepaid Virgin Plus plan? Change to one of its monthly plans before that date, and the number stays yours. Come in and we’ll handle the move, or lay Lucky Mobile’s prepaid plans alongside it for a comparison.

No new home internet or TV in Ontario

Since January 14, 2026, Virgin Plus has not signed up new home internet or TV customers in Ontario. Virgin Plus says anyone already on the service can stay on the plan, pricing and equipment they have now. Setting up a home from scratch? Give us the address and we’ll see which Bell services reach it.

What each one comes with

Lucky Mobile

  • Pay first, use it after. Each month is paid for before it begins, so no bill arrives afterwards.
  • Easy in, easy out. You won’t be asked to pass a credit check or to sign up for a term.
  • No data overage charges. Ask us what happens to your speed once the full-speed data runs out.

Virgin Plus

  • Billed monthly. Virgin Plus is a Bell brand, and most of its plans include Member Benefits at no extra cost.
  • Where the perks are. The My Benefits app lists offers on food, shopping, travel and entertainment. You don’t collect points, and the offers change often.
  • Coming from another provider? Your number can come across with you.

Bell

  • Bell SmartPay. A phone’s price is split across 24 monthly payments, with nothing down and no interest. The plan must be an eligible 2-year one, and Bell checks your credit.
  • Device Return Option. Part of an eligible phone’s cost is held over until the two years of the term are up. Hand the phone back in good working condition then, or keep it and pay that part.
  • Trade-in. Ask whether the phone you have now can earn credit toward the next one.
  • Keeping your phone. You can move to a Bell plan without buying a new handset.

Home and work

Bell at home

Internet, Fibe TV, Satellite TV and a home phone line, on their own or bundled. Your address decides which of them you can have.

How home setup works

Bell for business

Small firms can set up Bell phones and mobile plans for their whole staff here. Business smartphones qualify for SmartPay as well: 24 monthly payments, nothing down, on an eligible 2-year plan and subject to Bell’s credit approval.

Say how many phones you need and bring your company details.
