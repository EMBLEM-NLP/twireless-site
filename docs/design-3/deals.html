Paying for a handset, and for service

How the providers differ

  • Bell SmartPay

    A handset can be paid off over 24 monthly instalments, with no down payment and no interest charged. Only an eligible 2-year Bell plan qualifies, and Bell approves each application on credit.

  • Device Return Option

    Part of an eligible Bell handset’s cost is deferred across the 2-year term. A handset handed back in good working condition clears that part; one you keep means paying it.

  • Paying ahead with Lucky Mobile

    You pay for each month at the start, and that is the end of it: nothing is billed afterwards. There is no credit check and no commitment beyond the month you have paid for.
