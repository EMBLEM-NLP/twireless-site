What Bell can connect

  • Bell Internet

    Home internet plans. Addresses that sit on Bell’s fibre network can have fibre.

  • Fibe TV

    Watch through an app on the Bell Streamer, Apple TV, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV or Google Chromecast: live channels, plus shows on demand. On a phone or tablet, select on-demand movies and series can be downloaded for watching offline. Fibe TV needs Bell Internet.

  • Satellite TV

    Bell television that arrives through a satellite dish.

  • Home Phone

    A landline for the house. Whether you can have it without internet or TV depends on what Bell offers at your address.

Got Virgin Plus internet or TV already?

Since January 14, 2026, Virgin Plus has not taken new home internet or TV customers in Ontario. Bell says existing customers keep the plan, pricing and equipment they have now. Starting fresh? Tell us the address and we’ll see what Bell can offer there.
