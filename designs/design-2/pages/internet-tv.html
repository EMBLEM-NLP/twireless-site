Services Bell can connect

  • Bell Internet

    Home internet plans, with fibre available at addresses on Bell's fibre network.

  • Fibe TV

    Watch channels as they air or pick a show on demand, in the Fibe TV app. You need Bell Internet to use it.

  • Satellite TV

    TV from Bell through a satellite dish.

  • Home Phone

    A landline for the house, by itself or in a bundle with internet and TV.

Fibe TV through the app

These players work with the Fibe TV app: the Bell Streamer, Apple TV, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV and Google Chromecast.

  • Internet, TV and a home phone line can share one bill.

What happens at the counter

  1. Your address

    We find out what Bell can offer there.

  2. You choose

    You decide which of internet, TV and a landline you want, and we show how that sits alongside your mobile plan.

  3. Installation

    Sometimes a technician has to come to the house. If so, we set the appointment and explain what the visit involves.
