Bell

Bell's own monthly plans, with iPhone and Android phones to go with them. A phone doesn't have to be paid for in one go: Bell has two payment options, and we can arrange either at the counter.

  • Bell SmartPay. Split the phone's price into 24 monthly payments, with nothing to pay up front and no interest, if your 2-year plan qualifies. Bell checks your credit first.
  • Device Return Option. On eligible phones, part of the price is set aside until your 2-year term ends. Give the phone back in good working condition and that part is waived; keep it and it becomes payable.
  • Trading in. Got an old phone? Ask if it can earn credit toward the new one.
  • Happy with your phone? Keep using it and put a new plan on it.

Virgin Plus

Virgin Plus is a Bell brand too, sold on monthly plans. Most carry Member Benefits, a set of perks included in the price.

  • Member Benefits. Look in the My Benefits app for perks across food, shopping, travel and entertainment. You don't earn points, and the line-up of perks shifts over time.
  • Coming from another provider? Your number can come with you.

Last day for Virgin Plus prepaid: January 15, 2027. Still on Virgin Plus prepaid? Your number is safe as long as you're on a monthly plan by then. We can sort that out with you here, or set Lucky Mobile prepaid beside it so you can weigh them up.

Home internet and TV from Virgin Plus. Since January 14, 2026, new customers in Ontario can't sign up for Virgin Plus home internet or TV. Existing customers keep what they have. Setting up from scratch? Give us your address and we'll see which Bell services you can have.

Lucky Mobile

Lucky Mobile is Bell's prepaid brand: you pay first, then use the month you've paid for. No bill arrives afterwards.

  • Easy to start. Nobody checks your credit, and you aren't locked in.
  • Same network. You get the same Bell network as the other two brands.
  • Data without overage bills. Ask us what your speed does once the full-speed part of the plan runs out.

Bell home services

Internet, TV and a home phone line can be set up together or one at a time, wherever Bell's network reaches. Your address settles what's available.

  • Bell Internet. Home internet plans, with fibre available at addresses on Bell's fibre network.
  • Fibe TV. Watch channels as they air or pick something on demand, using the app. Players that work with it: Bell Streamer, Apple TV, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV and Google Chromecast. A phone or tablet can hold some recordings and on-demand picks for offline viewing. It only works with Bell Internet.
  • Satellite TV. Bell television received through a dish.
  • Home Phone. A landline for the house, by itself or in a bundle.
  • Bundles. More than one service? They can share a single bill.

Bell for business

Running a small business? Bell phones and mobile plans can be arranged for you and your staff here at the store.

  • Equipping staff. Business phones can use Bell SmartPay as well: 24 monthly payments, nothing down, on an eligible 2-year plan and subject to credit approval.
  • One place to ask. Come in and tell us how many phones your team needs and what they'll be used for. Bring your company details and we'll take it from there.

Each provider sets its own plans, prices and terms and can change them at any time, so check the current details with us before you commit.
