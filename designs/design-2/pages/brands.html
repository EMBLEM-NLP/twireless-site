Bell

Monthly plans on Bell's mobile network, with iPhone and Android phones. If you'd rather not pay for a new phone all at once, Bell has two ways to spread out the cost, and we can set up either one.

  • Bell SmartPay. Pay for your phone over 24 months with $0 down and 0% interest, on an eligible 2-year plan. Bell's credit approval applies.
  • Device Return Option. On eligible phones, part of the price is deferred. When your 2-year term ends, return the phone in good working condition, or keep it and pay the deferred amount.
  • Trade in. Ask whether your current phone qualifies for credit toward a new one.
  • Bring your own phone. Keep the phone you have and change only your plan.

Virgin Plus

Monthly mobile plans from Virgin Plus, a Bell brand. Most plans come with Member Benefits, a perks program included at no extra cost.

  • Member Benefits. Offers on entertainment, travel, shopping and food in the My Benefits app. There are no points to collect, and offers change often.
  • Switch and keep your number. Bring your number over from your current provider.

Virgin Plus prepaid ends January 15, 2027. If you're on a Virgin Plus prepaid plan, move to a monthly plan before then to keep your number. We can make the switch with you, or compare it with Lucky Mobile prepaid.

Virgin Plus internet and TV in Ontario: since January 14, 2026, Virgin Plus no longer takes new home internet or TV customers in Ontario. If you already have it, your plan, pricing and equipment stay the same. For a new home setup, we'll go through Bell home services with you.

Lucky Mobile

Lucky Mobile is Bell's prepaid brand. You pay before each month starts, so there's no bill afterwards.

  • No credit check and no long-term contract.
  • Bell's mobile network. Lucky Mobile plans run on the Bell network.
  • No data overage charges. Ask us how each plan handles data once you've used your full-speed amount.

Bell home services

Bell services for your home, on their own or together in a bundle. Bring your address, including any unit number, and we'll check what reaches it.

  • Bell Internet. Plans for your home, including fibre where Bell's fibre network reaches your address.
  • Fibe TV. Live and on-demand TV in the Fibe TV app, on the Bell Streamer, Apple TV, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV or Google Chromecast. You can download recordings and on-demand shows to watch on your phone or tablet. Fibe TV needs Bell Internet.
  • Satellite TV. Bell TV delivered by satellite dish.
  • Home Phone. A home phone line, on its own or with internet and TV.
  • Bundles. Combine services on one bill. Some bundles include Crave.
