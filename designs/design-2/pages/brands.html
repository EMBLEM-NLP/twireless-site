Bell

Bell's own monthly plans, with iPhone and Android phones to go with them. A phone doesn't have to be paid for in one go: Bell has two payment options, and we can arrange either at the counter.

  • Bell SmartPay. Pay for the phone in 24 monthly instalments with no money down and no interest, provided your 2-year plan is eligible. Bell approves your credit first.
  • Device Return Option. On eligible phones, you don't pay part of the price during your 2-year term. Return the phone in good working condition at the end and that part is waived. Keep the phone and you pay it then.
  • Trading in. Got an old phone? Ask if it can earn credit toward the new one.
  • Happy with your phone? Keep using it and put a new plan on it.

Virgin Plus

Virgin Plus is another Bell brand with monthly plans. Most of them include Member Benefits, perks that come with the plan at no added cost.

  • Member Benefits. Look in the My Benefits app for perks across food, shopping, travel and entertainment. You don't earn points, and the line-up of perks shifts over time.
  • Coming from another provider? Your number can come with you.

Virgin Plus prepaid ends on January 15, 2027. Still on Virgin Plus prepaid? Your number is safe as long as you're on a monthly plan by then. We can sort that out with you here, or set Lucky Mobile prepaid beside it so you can weigh them up.

Home internet and TV from Virgin Plus. Since January 14, 2026, new customers in Ontario can't sign up for Virgin Plus home internet or TV. Existing customers aren't affected: plan, price and equipment stay as they are. Setting up at home for the first time? We'll check what Bell offers at your address.

Lucky Mobile

Lucky Mobile is Bell's prepaid brand: you pay first, then use the month you've paid for. No bill arrives afterwards.

  • Easy to start. Nobody checks your credit, and you aren't locked in.
  • Same network. You get the same Bell network as the other two brands.
  • No overage charges for data. Ask us what happens to your speed once you've used the full-speed data in your plan.

Bell home services

Bell can set up internet, TV and a home phone line, together or one at a time. Tell us your address, with the unit number, and we'll see which of them reach your door.

  • Bell Internet. Home internet plans, with fibre available at addresses on Bell's fibre network.
  • Fibe TV. With the Fibe TV app you can watch channels as they air or pick something on demand. It runs on a Bell Streamer, Apple TV, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV or Google Chromecast. A phone or tablet can hold some recordings and on-demand picks for watching offline. Bell Internet is required.
  • Satellite TV. TV from Bell through a satellite dish.
  • Home Phone. A landline for the house, by itself or in a bundle.
  • Bundles. Get your services on a single bill. Some bundles also include Crave.

Bell for business

Got a small business? We can fit out you and your team with Bell phones and mobile plans.

  • Phones for the team. SmartPay works for business phones too: 24 monthly payments and nothing down, on an eligible 2-year plan and subject to credit approval.
  • One place to ask. Come in and tell us how many phones your team needs and what they'll be used for. Bring your company details and we'll take it from there.

Each provider sets its own plans, prices and terms and can change them at any time, so check the current details with us before you commit.
