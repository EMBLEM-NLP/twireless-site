What each one comes with

{{{ pic.card_bell }}}{{{ plate_photo.bell }}}

Bell

  • Bell SmartPay. A phone’s price is split across 24 monthly payments, with nothing down and no interest. The plan must be an eligible 2-year one, and Bell checks your credit. Leave the plan early and Bell asks for the balance still owing on the phone.
  • Device Return Option. Part of an eligible phone’s cost is held over until the two years of the term are up. Hand the phone back in good working condition then, or keep it and pay that part. Bell also calls this Flex Option.
  • Trade-in. Ask whether the phone you have now can earn credit toward the next one.
  • Keeping your phone. You can move to a Bell plan without buying a new handset.
{{{ pic.card_virgin }}}{{{ plate_photo.virgin_plus }}}

Virgin Plus

  • Billed monthly. Virgin Plus is a Bell brand, and most of its plans include Member Benefits at no extra cost.
  • Where the perks are. The My Benefits app lists offers on food, shopping, travel and entertainment. You don’t collect points, and the offers change often.
  • Coming from another provider? Your number can come across with you.
{{{ pic.card_lucky }}}{{{ plate_photo.lucky_mobile }}}

Lucky Mobile

  • Pay first, use it after. Each month is paid for before it begins, so no bill arrives afterwards.
  • Easy in, easy out. You won’t be asked to pass a credit check or to sign up for a term.
  • No data overage charges. Ask us what happens to your speed once the full-speed data runs out.
