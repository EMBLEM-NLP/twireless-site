Your details and what we do with them

The short version: we use what you type into our forms to get back to you, and for nothing else.

What we receive

The visit and repair forms send us what you type into them: your name, your phone number and your other answers. We don't use cookies to advertise or to track visitors on this site.

What we do with it

It's used to phone you back and to arrange your visit or repair. We never sell it or hand it to marketers.

Where it goes

Requests reach the store through the form service of our website host. Once a request has done its job, we delete it.

Your provider account

Anything you buy from Bell, Virgin Plus or Lucky Mobile is governed by the privacy policy of that provider.
