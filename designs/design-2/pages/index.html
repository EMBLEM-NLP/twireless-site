Switching? Keep your number

In Canada you can keep your phone number when you change providers. Moving to Bell, Virgin Plus or Lucky Mobile takes three steps.

  1. Bring what we need

    Government-issued photo ID and your current account number, which is on your bill.

  2. Choose your plan and phone

    We compare options from all three providers with you.

  3. We move your number

    Don't cancel your old plan first. Moving your number ends that service for you.
