Moving from another provider?

Moving to us? You keep your phone number, and we handle the transfer. Here's what happens when you come in.

  1. Your latest bill

    Bring a recent bill that shows your account number, and photo ID in case we need it.

  2. Choose what you'd like

    Over the counter, we compare the three providers and the phones you're considering.

  3. We transfer your number

    Leave your old plan running. When the number moves over, that service closes by itself.
