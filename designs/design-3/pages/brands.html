Bell

A monthly plan, and a new phone to go with it.

How you pay
Billed monthly.
What it adds
iPhone and Android phones, with ways to spread what a phone costs. The Phones page explains each.
Also from Bell
Home internet, TV and a home phone line, depending on the address (see Home services). A company can also ask about plans for its employees’ phones.

Virgin Plus

Monthly plans with perks included.

How you pay
Billed monthly.
What it adds
Most plans include Member Benefits: deals on travel, entertainment, food and shopping, found in the My Benefits app. You do not collect points, and the deals on offer now may not be the ones next month.
Worth knowing
Virgin Plus prepaid plans end on January 15, 2027. Moving to a monthly plan before then keeps your number, and we can make the move in the store.

Lucky Mobile

Pay ahead, and no bill follows.

How you pay
A month in advance, so there is never a bill to settle later.
What it adds
No credit check, no commitment beyond the month you have paid for, and no overage charges for data.
Worth knowing
Ask how a plan behaves once its full-speed data allowance is used.
