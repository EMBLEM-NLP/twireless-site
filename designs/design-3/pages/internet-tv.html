What the address may allow

Bell Internet
An internet connection for the household, with fibre at addresses covered by Bell’s fibre network.
Fibe TV

Through the Fibe TV app, a screen can show live channels and on-demand programs. A Bell Internet connection is required.

The app is available on a Bell Streamer, Apple TV, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV and Google Chromecast.

Offline viewing is supported: selected programs you have recorded, and shows saved from the on-demand list, can be played on a phone or tablet with no connection.

Satellite TV
Television from Bell, delivered by satellite to a dish.
Home Phone
A fixed line into the house, available as the only service or beside internet and TV.

Once we know what reaches you

Take any combination of internet, TV and a home phone line, and we will show how it sits beside your mobile plan. Where a technician has to install it, we make the booking and explain what to expect on the day.

Already have Virgin Plus at home? According to Virgin Plus, you may carry on as you are, with the same plan, price and equipment. Since January 14, 2026, a household in Ontario that is not already a customer cannot start Virgin Plus home internet or TV, so a new home is set up with Bell services instead.
