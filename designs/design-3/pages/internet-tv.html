Four ways to connect the household

  • Bell Internet

    An internet connection for the household, with fibre at addresses covered by Bell’s fibre network.

  • Fibe TV

    Through the Fibe TV app, a screen can show live channels and on-demand programs. A Bell Internet connection is required.

  • Satellite TV

    Television from Bell, delivered by satellite to a dish.

  • Home Phone

    A traditional landline for the home, ordered alone or alongside internet and TV.

Which devices run Fibe TV

The Fibe TV app is available on a Bell Streamer, Apple TV, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV and Google Chromecast.

  • Offline viewing is supported: selected programs you have recorded, and shows saved from the on-demand list, can be played on a phone or tablet with no connection.

How a setup unfolds

  1. The address

    We check which services Bell can connect there.

  2. The selection

    Pick any combination of internet, TV and landline; we will also show how it fits with your mobile plan.

  3. Getting connected

    Where installation needs a technician, we will make the booking and tell you what to expect when they arrive.
