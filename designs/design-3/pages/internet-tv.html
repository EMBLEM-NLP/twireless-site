What the address may allow

Bell Internet
An internet connection for the household, with fibre at addresses covered by Bell’s fibre network.
Fibe TV

Television, live or on demand, comes through the Fibe TV app, which needs a Bell Internet connection.

The app runs on a Bell Streamer, Apple TV, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV and Google Chromecast.

On a phone or tablet, select movies and series from on-demand channels such as Crave can be downloaded and watched without a connection.

Satellite TV
Bell television delivered by satellite to a dish.
Home phone
A fixed line into the house. Bell decides, address by address, whether it can be had without internet or TV.

After the lookup

Once we know what reaches you, we go through the choices with you. Where a technician has to install it, we make the booking and explain what to expect on the day.

Have your full address ready, including the unit number, and bring the latest bill if you are moving from another company.

Already have Virgin Plus at home? Households that already have it are not affected, Bell says: the plan, the price and the equipment stay as they are. Since January 14, 2026, a household in Ontario that is not already a customer cannot start Virgin Plus home internet or TV, so a new home is set up with Bell services instead.
