Your details, in questions and answers

What you enter in a form is used to reach you about that request, and for no other purpose.

What do you receive when I send a form?

Your name, your telephone number and whatever else you type in. This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

How is it used?

We use it to call you and arrange the visit or repair you asked for. It is not sold, and it is not shared for marketing.

Where does it go?

Forms travel to the store through the form service run by the company that hosts this website. A request the store no longer needs is deleted.

Does anything load from another company?

Only the map, and only when you press Show the map. It then loads from Google, which has its own privacy policy. Until you press the button, nothing is requested from Google.

What about my plan with Bell, Virgin Plus or Lucky Mobile?

A plan or service bought from Bell, Virgin Plus or Lucky Mobile falls under that company’s own privacy policy, not this page.
