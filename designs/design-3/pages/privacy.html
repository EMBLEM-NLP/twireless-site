A short note on your details

What you enter in a form is used to reach you about that request, and for no other purpose.

What reaches us

When you send the visit form or the repair form, we receive your name, your telephone number and whatever else you enter. This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

Your details let us ring you and organise the visit or repair you asked for. They are not sold, and they are not shared for marketing.

Where it travels

Forms travel to the store by way of the form service run by the company that hosts this website. A request the store no longer needs is deleted.

Purchases from the providers

A plan or service bought from Bell, Virgin Plus or Lucky Mobile falls under that company’s own privacy policy, not this notice.
