Every photograph has been cropped and resized, with a matching colour grade applied. Some carried small printed brand names, logos or labels; these were retouched out, and the entry for each says exactly what was removed. The Bell, Virgin Plus and Lucky Mobile logos are set on pearl-coloured mount cards beside the photographs, never on them. The engine-turned patterns were drawn for this website from a formula, so they carry no licence. The names and logos of Bell, Virgin Plus and Lucky Mobile belong to those companies.
