Where are you starting from?

Most visits begin in one of four places. Find yours and we will take it from there.

  • You want a new handset

    Telephone first and we will tell you whether the model is in the store; we carry both iPhone and Android handsets. Bell SmartPay spreads a handset’s price over 24 monthly payments with no interest, provided your 2-year plan is an eligible one and Bell approves your credit.

  • You like the phone you have

    A plan can be fitted to the phone already in your pocket. It may also be worth something against the next one; ask us how a trade-in works.

  • You are leaving another provider

    Changing provider need not mean a new number, because you can keep your number. Bring a recent bill, which carries the account number the transfer needs, and photo ID in case it is asked for. Please do not cancel the old plan yourself: the transfer ends it once the number has gone across.

  • You are equipping a team

    Handsets and mobile plans from Bell can be put together here for a small team. A telephone call or a visit is all it takes to begin. Read about Bell for business.
