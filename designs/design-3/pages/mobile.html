  3. Choose how to pay

    With a Bell plan, a new phone can be paid for in either of two ways.

    Bell SmartPay

    Over two years, the phone is paid off in 24 monthly payments. There is no down payment, and no interest is charged. Only an eligible 2-year Bell plan qualifies, and Bell decides each application on credit. End the agreement or the plan early and the balance still owing on the phone must be paid.

    Device Return Option

    A share of an eligible Bell phone’s price is held over for the 2-year term. If the phone then comes back in good working condition, that share is waived; if you keep it, you pay it. Some Bell plans call this Flex Option instead, and the terms differ from plan to plan.

  4. Keep your number

    Changing provider does not mean changing number. Bring a recent bill, since the account number printed on it is what the transfer needs, and photo ID in case it is asked for.

    Leave your old plan in place. The transfer closes it once your number has moved.
