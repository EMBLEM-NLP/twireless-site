Finding the right handset

Thinking of a particular model? Telephone first and we will tell you whether it is in the store; both iPhone and Android handsets are available.

  • Instalments. Bell SmartPay carries a handset’s price as 24 monthly payments with no interest, provided your 2-year plan is an eligible one.
  • Your current handset. It may be worth something against the next one; ask us how a trade-in works.
  • No new handset required. A plan can be fitted to the phone already in your pocket.

Joining us from another provider

Changing provider need not mean a new number, since you can keep your number. We handle the transfer with you in the store, in three steps.

  1. A bill and photo ID

    The account number on the bill is needed for the transfer. We may also ask for photo ID, so please bring it.

  2. Weigh the choices

    Each provider’s plans are laid beside the handsets you like, and you choose at your own pace.

  3. Completing the move

    Do not cancel the old plan beforehand. The transfer brings that service to an end once the number has gone across.
