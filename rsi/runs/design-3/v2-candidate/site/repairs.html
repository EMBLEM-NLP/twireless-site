Repairs, with the price agreed first

Cracked glass, a battery that gives out early, a port that will not charge: phones and tablets are both welcome, and every repair is priced before it begins.

Request a repair Get a quotation by telephone

How a repair is agreed

Before any work

We examine the device, find the fault and name a price. Until you have agreed to it, nothing is touched.

When it is ready

You are given a time to collect the device, and you are welcome to switch it on and try it at the counter before you take it home.

What can be mended

Each model and each part costs differently, so we quote every repair before work begins.

  • Cracked screens and glass

    New glass or a new screen for a phone or tablet that has taken a fall.

  • Tired batteries

    For a phone that is flat by lunchtime, shuts down with charge left, or has a battery that is visibly bulging.

  • Charging faults

    A charger that no longer seems to work, or a cable that must be held at a certain angle before the phone will charge.

  • Camera and rear panel

    A scratched or split camera lens, pictures that come out soft, or a rear panel that has shattered.

  • Speaker and microphone

    A caller you can barely hear, a speaker that has gone thin, or a microphone that no longer picks you up.

  • After a soaking

    A handset that has been soaked should be powered down at once and brought to us promptly.

Tell us about the fault

Tell us what is wrong with the device, and we will ring you to fix a time to bring it in. You can also walk in every day from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m.
