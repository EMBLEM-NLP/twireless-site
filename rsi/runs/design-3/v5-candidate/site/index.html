Before you visit

Something else? Call 647-707-1093 and ask the store directly.

Is an appointment needed?

No. You are welcome every day from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. Booking a visit is optional; it only means we expect you.

Whose plans do you sell?

Those of Bell, Virgin Plus and Lucky Mobile. As a Bell Authorised Retailer we also arrange Bell home internet, TV and a home phone line.

Can I bring my own phone?

Yes. Bring the phone you have and we will set the plan up on it. A new phone is optional.

What happens to my number if I change provider?

It moves with you. Bring a recent bill, since the account number printed on it is what the transfer needs, and photo ID in case it is asked for. Leave the old plan in place: the transfer closes it.

Is the entrance wheelchair accessible?

The entrance has wheelchair access. Please call before you come if you need anything else arranged.
