Moving your number

Your number can go with you to a new provider, and the move is handled with you in the store.

Bring along

  • A recent bill. The account number printed on it is what the move requires.
  • Photo ID, which may be asked for as well.

What follows

Your existing plan stays active until the number has gone across. The transfer then closes it, so please do not cancel it yourself.

Questions we are often asked

If your question is not here, ring the store on 647-707-1093 and ask it directly.

Whose plans do you sell?

Those of Bell, Virgin Plus and Lucky Mobile. As a Bell Authorised Retailer we also arrange Bell home internet, TV and a home phone line.

Can I bring my own phone?

Yes. Bring the phone you have and we will set the plan up on it; a new handset is optional.

Must I book ahead?

No. You are welcome every day from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. Booking is optional; it simply lets us expect you.

What becomes of my number if I change provider?

It goes with you: in Canada, changing provider does not oblige you to change number. The account number printed on a recent bill is what the transfer needs, so please bring one, along with photo ID in case it is requested. Leave your existing plan untouched until the transfer is complete; the move itself brings that service to an end.

Can a tablet be repaired too?

Yes, a tablet is as welcome as a phone. Once we know the model and the fault, we will quote you a price; work begins only if you agree.

Is the entrance suitable for a wheelchair?

Wheelchair users can come in by the store’s accessible entrance. For another access question, ring us before you visit.
