Repairs, quoted first

Bring in a phone or a tablet. We find the fault, name the price, and begin only when you agree.

Request a repair Call for a quote

  1. Bring it in

    Phones and tablets are both welcome whenever the store is open. If you can, back up your photos and files first.

  2. Agree the price

    We examine the device, find the fault and name a price. Until you have agreed to it, nothing is touched.

  3. Collect it

    You are given a time to collect the device, and you can switch it on and try it at the counter before you take it home.

What we mend

  • Cracked screens and glass

    New glass or a new screen for a phone or tablet that has taken a fall.

  • Tired batteries

    A phone that is flat by lunchtime, shuts down with charge left, or has a battery that is visibly bulging.

  • Charging faults

    A charger that no longer works, or a cable that must be held at an angle before the phone will charge.

  • Camera and rear panel

    A scratched or split lens, soft pictures, or a rear panel that has shattered.

  • Speaker and microphone

    A caller you can barely hear, a speaker that has gone thin, or a microphone that no longer picks you up.

  • After a soaking

    Switch the device off straight away, and bring it to us promptly.

Tell us about the fault

We will call to fix a time for you to bring the device in. You can also walk in every day from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m..
