Questions we hear a lot

Do I need an appointment?

No. You can walk in every day from 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. If you book a visit, we can set time aside for you.

Can I keep my number if I switch providers?

Yes. In Canada you can keep your number when you change providers. Bring photo ID and your current account number, and don't cancel your old plan first: moving your number ends that service for you.

Is T Wireless a Bell store?

We're a Bell Authorised Retailer. We sell Bell, Virgin Plus and Lucky Mobile plans and phones, and Bell internet, TV and home phone.

Can you fix my phone or tablet?

We repair screens, batteries, charging ports, cameras and more. Tell us your model and the problem, and we'll quote the repair before we start.

Is the store wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The store has a wheelchair-accessible entrance.
