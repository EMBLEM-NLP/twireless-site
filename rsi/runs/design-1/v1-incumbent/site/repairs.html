What we fix

  • Cracked screens

    Screen and glass replacement for phones and tablets.

  • Batteries

    For phones that drain fast, shut off early or have a swollen battery.

  • Charging ports

    When your phone won't charge or only charges at an angle.

  • Cameras and back glass

    Blurry photos, a cracked lens or a shattered back.

  • Speakers and microphones
