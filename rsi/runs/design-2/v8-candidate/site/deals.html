How you can pay for a phone

See how the three differ

  • Bell SmartPay

    The price is split across 24 months, with nothing down and 0% interest. You need an eligible 2-year Bell plan, and Bell checks your credit first.

  • Device Return Option

    On eligible Bell phones, part of the price can wait until your 2-year term ends. Hand the phone back in good working condition then, or keep it and pay that part.

  • Lucky Mobile prepaid

    Sign up without a credit check or a contract. Each month is paid for before you use it, so nothing is billed later.
