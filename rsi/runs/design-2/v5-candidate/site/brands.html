Bell, Virgin Plus or Lucky Mobile? Here's the difference

All three run on Bell's mobile network, so the choice comes down to how you pay and what comes with the plan. Here's the short version, then the detail. Still unsure? Bring your last bill and we'll go through it together.

Bell

Bell's own monthly plans, with iPhone and Android phones to go with them. A phone doesn't have to be paid for in one go: Bell has two payment options, and we can arrange either at the counter.

  • Bell SmartPay. The phone's price is split across 24 months: nothing down, 0% interest, on an eligible 2-year plan. Bell's credit approval applies.
  • Device Return Option. On eligible phones, part of the price is held back until your 2-year term is over. Return the phone in good working condition and you don't pay that amount. Keep it, and you do.
  • Trading in. Got an old phone? Ask if it can earn credit toward the new one.
  • Happy with your phone? Keep using it and put a new plan on it.

Virgin Plus

A Bell brand that sells monthly mobile plans. Most Virgin Plus plans include Member Benefits, a perks program you don't pay extra for.

  • Member Benefits. Perks on food, shopping, travel and entertainment, found in the My Benefits app. You don't collect points, and the offers keep changing.
  • Coming from another provider? Your number can come with you.

Virgin Plus prepaid ends on January 15, 2027. Still on Virgin Plus prepaid? Switch to a monthly plan before that date and your number stays with you. We can make the move with you, or line it up against Lucky Mobile prepaid.

Home internet and TV from Virgin Plus. Since January 14, 2026, new customers in Ontario can't sign up for Virgin Plus home internet or TV. Current customers keep their plan, price and equipment. Setting up at home for the first time? We'll look at Bell's home services together.

Lucky Mobile

Lucky Mobile is Bell's prepaid brand: you pay first, then use the month you've paid for. No bill arrives afterwards.

  • Easy to start. Sign up without a credit check or a long-term contract.
  • Same network. You get the same Bell network as the other two brands.
  • No overage charges for data. Ask us what happens to your speed once you've used the full-speed data in your plan.

Bell home services

Bell can set up internet, TV and a home phone line, together or one at a time. Tell us your address, with the unit number, and we'll see which of them reach your door.

  • Bell Internet. Home internet plans, with fibre available at addresses on Bell's fibre network.
  • Fibe TV. Watch live TV and on-demand shows through the Fibe TV app, on a Bell Streamer, Apple TV, Android TV, Amazon Fire TV or Google Chromecast. Shows you've recorded or picked on demand can be downloaded to a phone or tablet. Fibe TV needs Bell Internet.
  • Satellite TV. TV from Bell through a satellite dish.
  • Home Phone. A landline for the house, by itself or in a bundle.
  • Bundles. Get your services on a single bill. Some bundles also include Crave.

Bell for business

Got a small business? We can fit out you and your team with Bell phones and mobile plans.

  • Phones for the team. Business phones can use SmartPay too: 24 months to pay, nothing down, with an eligible 2-year plan.
  • One place to ask. Bring your company details to the store and we'll talk through plans for your staff.

Each provider sets its own plans, prices and terms and can change them at any time, so check the current details with us before you commit.
