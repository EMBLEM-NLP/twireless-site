Before you set out

Choosing a phone or plan
Bring photo ID, in case we need it to set up a plan. Have one model in mind? Ring ahead and we'll check it's in.
Moving your number
Bring a recent bill: it carries your account number. Leave the old plan alone, because it closes by itself once the number has moved.
Dropping off a repair
Bring the phone or tablet. If you can, copy its photos and files somewhere safe first.
Buying for a team
Say how many phones you need, and bring your company’s details.
